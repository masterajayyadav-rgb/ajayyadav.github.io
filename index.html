Hi, I'm Ajay.

Strategic Procurement Professional — 9 years across maritime, chemical, and technology procurement.

Who I Am

I blend category management and supplier negotiation with data analytics, automation, and AI-driven solutions — commercial strategy and tech-driven efficiency working as one, not as separate skills, to optimize how a global supply chain actually runs.

What I Do

At A.P. Moller – Maersk, I manage procurement across four categories — Lubricants, Paints, Chemicals, and Reefer — supporting a 48-vessel Maersk-owned fleet, external clients, newbuilding projects, and Maersk Tankers projects. My work spans strategic sourcing, supplier relationship management, cost optimization, and cross-functional collaboration.

Beyond Procurement

I proactively build digital solutions that improve operational visibility — dynamic HTML dashboards for urgent logistics orders, OS&D cases, EAL dry-dock readiness, and invoice tracking, plus supplier-data scraping tools covering five suppliers' lubricant availability and lead times. I also built AJ GPT, an AI assistant that organizes project timelines, summarizes emails, tracks pending tasks, and monitors invoice and order status.

Philosophy

I am passionate about bridging procurement expertise and technology — turning manual processes into smarter workflows, complex data into actionable insights, and operational challenges into scalable solutions.

Beyond the Office
